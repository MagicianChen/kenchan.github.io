I am a third-year **PhD student in Computer Science** at the [University of Macau](https://www.um.edu.mo/){:target="_self"}, where I work under the guidance of [Prof. Derek F. Wong](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/personal/derek-wong/){:target="_self"} at the [NLP2CT](https://nlp2ct.cis.umac.mo/){:target="_self"} Lab. I'm passionate about creating **AI applications** that bring research ideas to life and help **thousands of users worldwide**. My research focuses on three main areas: **Personalized Large Language Models**, **LLM Agents**, and **Multi-Modal RAG systems**. I'm particularly interested in building **smarter AI systems** that can understand individual users better and provide more helpful responses. My work includes developing **personalized chatbots** and **intelligent assistants** that can learn from different types of information to better serve users in **education and travel**.

As a **researcher who loves building real applications**, I actively participate in [**competitions and research challenges**](../competitions/){:target="_self"} to sharpen my skills and test new ideas. My goal is to bridge the gap between **academic research and practical solutions** that people can actually use. I've developed **smart education platforms** and **automated travel planning tools** that show how research can make a real difference in people's lives. You can check out my [**AI applications**](../applications){:target="_self"} to see how I turn research concepts into useful tools that solve real problems.
