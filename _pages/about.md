I am a year four **PhD student in Computer Science** at the [University of Macau](https://www.um.edu.mo/){:target="_self"}, advised by [Prof. Derek F. Wong](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/personal/derek-wong/){:target="_self"} at the [NLP2CT](https://nlp2ct.cis.umac.mo/){:target="_self"} Lab. My [**research**](../publications){:target="_self"} focuses on Personalized Large Language Models, LLM Agents, and Multi-Modal RAG systems. I serve as a reviewer for ACL and EMNLP. I'm particularly interested in building AI systems that can better understand individual users and deliver more helpful responses.

Beyond research, I enjoy building practical applications. I actively participate in [**competitions and algorithm challenges**](../competitions/){:target="_self"} to test new ideas. I've developed smart education platforms and automated travel planning tools that demonstrate how research can make a real difference. Check out my [**AI applications**](../applications){:target="_self"} to see how I turn research into tools that solve real problems.
