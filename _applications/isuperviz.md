## Application Overview

**AI Research Assistant Platform** is an intelligent academic research solution that integrates AI-powered paper tracking with comprehensive reference management, research overlap detection, and hallucination verification capabilities, enabling academics, graduate students, and research institutions to streamline their entire research workflow—from literature discovery to manuscript validation—through simple natural language commands. The platform leverages advanced large language models and scholarly data mining to automatically monitor and curate newly published papers across targeted research domains, intelligently organize and cross-reference bibliographic materials, proactively identify research overlaps and potential scooping risks by analyzing similarities between a user's in-progress work and emerging publications, and rigorously detect AI-generated hallucinations in academic writing by cross-validating claims against verified scholarly sources—while featuring dynamic research dashboards that adapt monitoring priorities and alert thresholds based on evolving research interests and project timelines, revolutionizing academic productivity through intuitive conversational interaction and delivering personalized research intelligence across university research labs, doctoral programs, and independent scholarly pursuits.

## Features

- An AI-powered research assistant platform that leverages large language models to provide intelligent paper tracking, automated reference organization, and personalized research monitoring experiences
- Features research overlap and scooping risk detection, AI hallucination identification in academic writing, and real-time scholarly alert analytics
- Technologies: LLMs Agents, Multi-modal RAG, Citation Network Analysis, Semantic Similarity Detection
